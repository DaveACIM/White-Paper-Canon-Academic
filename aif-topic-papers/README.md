
## AIF Topic Papers — Purpose & Structure

The AI Fellowship (AIF) Topic Papers translate the core principles of the White Paper Canon Academic (WPCA) into focused, applied, and testable domains.

Where WPCA establishes the foundational architecture — coherence-first intelligence, unified causality, epistemic integrity, and system-level stability — the AIF Topic Papers:

• explore specific failure modes in human and artificial intelligence  
• demonstrate how coherence constraints resolve drift, hallucination, and fragmentation  
• bridge theory into practical design, assessment, and governance implications  
• serve as modular research units that can be independently read, shared, and implemented  

Together, WPCA defines the structural laws of coherent intelligence, while the AIF Topic Papers operationalize those laws across real-world contexts.

To maintain clarity and long-term scalability, Topic Papers are organized into thematic suites.
